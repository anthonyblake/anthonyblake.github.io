## Event

Next week I am attending the Power Platform Community Conference in Las Vegas. The main conference takes place over 3 days and there are countless talks, sessions and opportunities to discuss the latest Power Platform features and, of course, Agents and AI. In a time when the industry is moving and changing at an incredible pace, this conference is more important than ever.

## Sessions

As a Technical Architect, who has been focussed recently on agentic governance, the sessions I am most excited for at this conference relate to Agent 365, the new control plane for agentic AI. Applying familiar RBAC and identity to Agents with Agent ID is important now, but will be essential when organisations are running vast numbers of automated agents in the future. Least privilege and zero trust principles are just as applicable to agents as they are to regular users.
